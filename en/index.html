Frequently asked questions

Do I need a running watch?

No. You can log your runs manually in the app. If you have a watch or use your phone, you can connect it and import everything automatically.

Can I log runs manually?

Yes, without limits, on every plan — including Free, and even offline.

Can I use Strava?

Yes. Strava is the first integration available and imports distance, time, pace, heart rate, elevation, cadence, splits and route.

Are integrations paid?

No. Connecting Strava or any other available platform is free on every plan, and activity import is unlimited.

Does the plan change automatically?

It depends on the plan. On Free you request a review whenever you want. On Pro your week is adapted automatically. On Performance the plan evolves after every run.

Can I buy more AI usage?

Yes. There are one-off Extra AI packs for anyone who wants to chat and analyze more without changing plans.
