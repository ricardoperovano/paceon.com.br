Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, write to contato@paceon.com.br — we reply on business days.

Do I need a running watch?

No. You can log your runs manually in the app. If you have a watch or use your phone, you can connect it and import everything automatically.

Can I log runs manually?

Yes, without limits, on every plan — including Free, and even offline.

Can I use Strava?

Yes. Strava is the first integration available and imports distance, time, pace, heart rate, elevation, cadence, splits and route.

Are integrations paid?

No. Connecting Strava or any other available platform is free on every plan, and activity import is unlimited.

Does the plan change automatically?

It depends on the plan. On Free you request a review whenever you want. On Pro your week is adapted automatically. On Performance the plan evolves after every run.

Can I buy more AI usage?

Yes. There are one-off Extra AI packs for anyone who wants to chat and analyze more without changing plans.

Do I need to upgrade to use more AI?

Not necessarily. You can buy extra packs. The subscription is what unlocks exclusive features; packs extend how much you use.

Do extra credits expire?

No. Purchased credits never expire and are used after the credits included in your subscription.

Can I cancel?

Yes, whenever you want. You keep the features until the end of the paid period and your history stays saved.

Do you charge per kilometer?

No. You never pay for what you run. You pay for the level of intelligence the product delivers: analysis, adaptation and depth of insight.

I've never run. Is the plan for me?

It is. Choosing "start running" as your goal, the plan is born alternating running and walking — in the first week, blocks of 1 minute running and 2 walking — and increases the running time gradually. Within 8 weeks most people complete 5 km without stopping.

Can I train for a race with a set date?

Yes. When you add the race, periodization is built backwards from its date, with base, build, peak and taper spread over the available time. If the window is too short for the long run to reach the ideal, the app tells you beforehand and suggests alternatives instead of cramming the plan.

Can I move a workout to another day?

You can. In the plan grid, drag the workout to another day or adjust the distance on the card itself. The safety rules still apply: if the change would put two hard workouts on consecutive days, the app warns you.

How do you calculate my pace ranges?

From your actual pace on easy runs over recent weeks, plus the best time you entered, if any. Each workout type gets a range derived from that reference — easy runs sit 60 to 90 seconds per kilometer slower than 10K pace, tempo runs 10 to 20 seconds slower, and so on. As you run, the reference updates.

Do you build diets?

No. PaceOn builds and adjusts training. We don't diagnose, prescribe or give nutritional guidance — for that, see a health professional or a sports dietitian.

Can I use it alongside my coach?

You can. Many people use the app to keep the plan organized, the history analyzed and the adjustments happening between conversations, while keeping in-person coaching for running form and injuries.

How long it takes to get your plan

Generating it takes less than a minute. If you connected Strava, the plan is calibrated from what you actually ran over the last weeks: real volume, average easy pace and longest recent run. Without imported history it starts from your questionnaire answers — and adjusts as soon as the first runs come in.

The plan covers from the current week to your race date, when there is one. With no race booked, the length comes from your goal: 8 weeks to start running or improve a 5K, 10 to 12 for a 10K, 12 to 16 for a half, 16 to 20 for a marathon.

What happens when you miss a workout

Nothing piles up. The workout that didn't happen is marked as missed and the following week is recalculated from what you actually ran — not from what was on paper. That's the difference between a spreadsheet and a coach: nobody tells you to run 60 km this week because 20 were missing last week.

If you log the run later, it still matches the planned session: the search allows a one-day window on each side, so a Sunday long run done on Monday still closes the right session.

Your data and the artificial intelligence

We send the model only a summary of your sports context: goal, volume over recent weeks, reference pace, perceived effort and a short list of recent runs. We don't send your full history, your name, your email or the route of your runs.

Access tokens for the platforms you connect are stored encrypted and used only to import activities. You can disconnect, export your data and delete your account at any time from the app settings.

Where PaceOn runs

It's an installable web app (PWA): open it in your phone, tablet or desktop browser and add it to the home screen on Android and iPhone, where it behaves like a regular app — including offline, to log runs when the connection drops. There's no store to download from and no update to install.

If you're still unsure how the planning side works, the how it works page details the process and the pricing page shows what changes between Free, Pro and Performance. The technical terms that show up in your plan are explained in the glossary, and the pace calculator works without signing up.
