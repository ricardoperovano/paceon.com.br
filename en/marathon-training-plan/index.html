Marathon training plan

42.195 km doesn't forgive improvisation. A well-built marathon cycle takes 16 to 20 weeks, with disciplined volume progression and recovery taken seriously.

How the week is organized

A plan isn't a list of runs: every workout has a role.

Long base

Many weeks of aerobic volume. In the marathon, base is what decides the last 10 km.

Progressive long runs

Growth of at most 2 km at a time, with down weeks every three or four weeks.

Marathon pace

Long blocks at target pace, which also train your nutrition and hydration strategy.

Three-week taper

Gradual volume reduction while keeping short race-pace stimuli.

Costly mistakes

  • Running long runs beyond 32 km thinking it "guarantees" the race.
  • Practicing nutrition only on race day.
  • Ignoring signs of accumulated fatigue and persistent pain.
  • Starting the race faster than the pace you trained.

Common questions

What's the longest long run in a marathon plan?

In most plans, between 30 and 32 km. Going beyond that raises injury risk without a proportional gain.

How many runs a week?

4 to 6, depending on your history. Consistency over months matters more than an isolated volume peak.

Can I train for a marathon as a beginner?

It's possible, but the responsible path goes through finishing a 10K and a half marathon first. The plan is built from your real starting point.
